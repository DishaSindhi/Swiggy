# Swiggy Sales Analysis using SQL

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **Swiggy’s food delivery sales data** using SQL queries. The objective was to extract meaningful insights about customers, restaurants, orders, and delivery partners. By applying different SQL techniques such as filtering, aggregations, and joins, the project provides a structured way to understand customer behavior, restaurant performance, and operational efficiency.

---

##  Objectives

* Analyze customer order behavior and activity.
* Evaluate restaurant ratings and revenue performance.
* Track delivery partner efficiency and workload distribution.
* Demonstrate SQL skills through real-world business use cases.

---

##  Key SQL Concepts Used

* **Database Creation & Management**
* **SQL Joins (INNER, LEFT, RIGHT, FULL, CROSS, SELF JOIN)**
* **Aggregations (AVG, COUNT, SUM, GROUP BY, HAVING)**
* **Filtering & Sorting (WHERE, ORDER BY, LIKE, BETWEEN)**

---

##  Problem Statements & Solutions

1. Display all customers who live in *Delhi*.
2. Find the **average rating** of all restaurants in *Mumbai*.
3. List customers who have placed at least one order.
4. Display the **total number of orders** placed by each customer.
5. Calculate the **total revenue generated** by each restaurant.
6. Find the **Top 5 restaurants** with the highest average rating.
7. Display all customers who have **never placed an order**.
8. Find the number of orders placed by each customer in *Mumbai*.
9. Display all **orders placed in the last 30 days**.
10. List delivery partners who have completed more than 1 delivery.
11. Find customers who placed orders on **exactly three different days**.
12. Identify the delivery partner who has worked with the **most different customers**.
13. Find customers from the same city who ordered from the same restaurants but on **different dates**.

---

##  Insights Gained

* Identified **active vs inactive customers** based on order history.
* Evaluated **restaurant performance** using ratings and revenue.
* Analyzed **customer loyalty patterns** based on repeated orders.
* Found the **most engaged delivery partners**.
